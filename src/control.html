MangNotch v2.0.0

Interactive Living Notch & Productivity Companion dengan sentuhan estetika editorial MangNime.

Rust Backend Tauri v2 Liquid Glass UI Open Source
Media, Volume & Live Lyrics Integrasi Windows GSMTC, kontrol master volume real-time, warna album, dan lirik.
Native File Shelf Simpan file sementara via Drag-in dari Explorer & Drag-out langsung ke WhatsApp/Discord.
Focus, Hydration & Tasks Timer Pomodoro kustom, pencatat hidrasi auto-reset harian, & To-Do List dual style.
System Peeks & Proximity Pop-up interaktif Volume, Brightness, Bluetooth, Caps Lock, Charger & auto-shrink.
